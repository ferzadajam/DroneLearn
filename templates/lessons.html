LESSON 01 - BEGINNER

Introduction to Drones

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Explain what a drone is.
  • Identify the main parts of a drone.
  • Describe the four basic drone movements.

1. What Is a Drone?

A drone is an aircraft that operates without a pilot on board. Many drones are controlled remotely, while others can follow programmed flight instructions.

2. Main Parts of a Drone

⚙️

Motors

Spin the propellers to produce thrust.

🌀

Propellers

Move air to help the drone lift and fly.

🔋

Battery

Provides electrical power to the drone.

🎮

Flight Controller

Processes commands and helps stabilize flight.

🛠️

Frame

Supports and connects the drone components.

3. How Does a Drone Move?

Throttle

Controls upward and downward movement.

Yaw

Rotates the drone left or right.

Pitch

Tilts the drone forward or backward.

Roll

Tilts the drone left or right.

LESSON 02 - BEGINNER

Drone Safety & Flight Rules

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Identify important drone safety rules.
  • Complete a basic pre-flight safety check.
  • Explain how to respond to unsafe flight conditions.

1. Basic Safety Rules

👀

Keep Your Drone in Sight

Always watch your drone while flying.

🚶

Stay Away from People

Keep a safe distance from people and obstacles.

🌦️

Check the Weather

Do not fly in rain, strong wind, or storms.

🔋

Check the Battery

Make sure the battery is charged and undamaged.

🧑‍🏫

Follow Instructor Directions

Never fly during class without permission.

2. Pre-Flight Checklist

  • Inspect the propellers and frame.
  • Check the battery level.
  • Make sure the flight area is clear.
  • Confirm that the controller is connected.
  • Get permission from your instructor.

3. What If Something Goes Wrong?

If you notice a problem during flight, stay calm. Follow your instructor's directions and land safely when possible. Never try to catch a moving drone with your hands.

LESSON 03 - BEGINNER

Drone Programming with Blockly

Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Explain what block-based programming is.
  • Identify basic CoDrone EDU flight commands.
  • Create a simple flight sequence using Blockly.

1. What Is Blockly?

Blockly is a visual programming system. Instead of typing code, students connect programming blocks to create instructions.

2. Basic Flight Commands

🚀

Take Off

Starts the drone's flight.

⬆️

Move Forward

Moves the drone forward.

↩️

Turn

Rotates the drone left or right.

⏳

Wait

Pauses before the next instruction.

🛬

Land

Ends the flight safely.
